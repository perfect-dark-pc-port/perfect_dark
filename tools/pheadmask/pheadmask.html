PerfectHead hair masks

Drop the files here, or use the buttons.
Brush 2

Left button paints hair (blue), right button paints skin. Hair takes the chosen hair colour in the game, skin the skin tone; both keep their original shading. On the face, the photo covers all the skin (unpainted texels) below the yellow hairrow line and inside the dotted square's sides and bottom, so the hair you paint there is where the photo stops. With "Mirror the sides" on, painting one side also paints the other. Textures are shown upright; the export flips them back. Save the export as src/game/pheadmasks.c and rebuild.
